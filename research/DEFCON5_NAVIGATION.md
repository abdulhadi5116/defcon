# DEFCON 5 naval permissions and weapon ranges

Latest user balance: fighter primary missile and ASW range 3 degrees; carrier AA and ASW range 5 degrees. Fighter guns remain 2 degrees. Passive-submarine target eligibility is unchanged and the 1.5-degree firing cap still overrides a larger normal weapon range. Existing launched projectiles retain their original travel budgets; new targeting, scopes and combat use the new reach.

Global Conflict naval movement at DEFCON 5 is restricted to active own/allied maritime territory. Simulation.seaPointAllowed uses the session's real territory map, including legacy six-faction map selection; seaSegmentAllowed checks intermediate positions. The navigation router accepts a per-call permission predicate for nodes, edges and smoothing. Coastline-only caches do not store alliance permissions. Formation routes evaluate every member's offset, and exact hull segments are checked before accepting the command.

Runtime movement rechecks permission, including saved paths and changing alliances. Friendly collision avoidance also checks the sea predicate, and validates nominal motion at DEFCON 5 so an unchanged velocity cannot bypass it. Formation-wide stopped translations keep one ship from leaving while others remain inside. A fleet whose old route becomes forbidden holds at the sea border rather than crossing it. DEFCON 4 removes this territorial restriction while retaining water/coast rules. Battle Lab remains an unrestricted exercise at its normal DEFCON 1.

Readiness copy and the field manual describe own/allied sea movement. An invalid movement order at DEFCON 5 explains the sea-territory requirement rather than reporting only open-water navigation.

Verification: 275 tests passed. Dedicated cases cover own versus enemy/neutral sea, active alliances, full-hull fit, unauthorized strips between legal endpoints, legal A* detours, revoked alliances on an existing path, collision avoidance at a sea border, DEFCON 4 release, and actual weapon acquisition/scope boundaries. Local Edge UI tests use real world territory masks: own North American and allied European fleet orders accepted, neutral Atlantic destination rejected at DEFCON 5 without replacing the previous order, then accepted at DEFCON 4. Fighter scopes show 3° missiles/2° guns; carrier shows 5°. Existing browser combat also verifies real launches, firing, bomber sortie recovery and city rendering. No browser errors. Safari was not tested.
