UP TO TEN TERRITORIES
COMMAND REFERENCE / 001

Before the silence.

Choose one of ten territories and configure alliances before taking command with classic v1.43 unit assets and movement parameters. Deploy a finite arsenal, launch aircraft from their hosts, and manage unit states through nuclear escalation.

01 / Deploy

During DEFCON 5–4, place your remaining ground units or compose a fleet of exactly six ships. Once all six fleet slots are filled, click directly in your maritime zone to place the fleet.

02 / Command

Left click to select. Right click the map to move the fleet or aircraft. At DEFCON 5, fleet routes must stay inside your own or allied sea zones. Open-sea movement unlocks at DEFCON 4. Right click a unit to change its state. Click an enemy icon or city for the selected unit’s primary action. Empty-map clicks retain selection. In fighter or bomber state, left click the map to launch from the selected airbase or carrier. For nuclear coordinates, press Target nuclear strike first. Space cancels coordinate targeting while keeping selection; Esc clears selection. Each launch click adds one order; additional targets queue through reload. Clear pending launches in Info to cancel the queue.

03 / Release

At DEFCON 1, switch a silo or submarine to nuclear launch, or choose a nuclear bomber payload at its carrier/airbase. Wait through preparation, then click an enemy surface icon or city. Use Target nuclear strike for coordinates. ICBM strike range is global; local radar coverage is available through the Radar layer. Silos in launch mode cannot provide air defense. After their nuclear payload is exhausted and the launch queue is empty, they automatically begin air-defense preparation.

04 / Survive

Aircraft consume fuel, return to a compatible host and land. Use Menu → Hand over to AI to let your territory operate autonomously. Take command in the header or menu to regain control; accepted orders continue. All factions use one strategic AI: complete deployment, reconnaissance, coordinated strike packages, synchronized arrivals and damage assessment. Reserves and defensive cover are managed according to detected threats. Default scoring: +2 per million enemy dead, −1 per million allied dead. The 45-minute victory timer starts when nuclear inventory reaches 20%.

Carrier: 5 fighters, 2 bombers, 6 SRBMs; fighter, bomber or ASW search state. Its only direct weapon is a short-range anti-aircraft gun; ASW search detects submarines without attacking them. Airbase: 5 fighters, 5 bombers, 10 SRBMs. Silo: 10 ICBMs, defense or launch. Submarine: 5 MRBMs, passive, active or surfaced launch. Bomber: one SRBM, naval or nuclear state. Radar discovers contacts; submerged submarines require sonar. Silo launches reveal their fixed installation. Fighter patrols and defensive silos can intercept detected descending warheads. Menu → Settings → Map intelligence switches Radar mode / Full view; full visibility does not extend weapons or sensor locks. DEFCON 3 lasts 30 game minutes; DEFCON 2 lasts 60 game minutes.
